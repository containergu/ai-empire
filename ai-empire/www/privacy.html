Privacy Policy — AI Empire TD

Last updated: August 30, 2026

AI Empire TD ("the Game") is a free casual tower-defense game. This policy explains what data is collected when you play.

1. Data we collect

The Game itself does not require an account and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or precise location.

2. Advertising

The Game displays advertisements served by third-party ad providers, including CrazyGames and Google AdMob. These providers may collect data to serve ads, such as your device type, IP address, advertising identifier, and coarse location. This data is collected and used by the ad providers under their own privacy policies:

  • CrazyGames: crazygames.com/privacy-policy
  • Google: policies.google.com/privacy

3. Local storage

The Game stores your progress (levels, stars, settings) locally on your device using browser local storage. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any server.

4. In-app purchases

The Game may offer an optional "Remove Ads" purchase. Purchases are processed by the respective platform (e.g., CrazyGames or Google Play) and are subject to that platform's terms and privacy policy.

5. Your choices

You can limit ad personalization or reset your advertising identifier through your device settings. You can also clear the Game's locally stored data through your browser settings.

6. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us.
