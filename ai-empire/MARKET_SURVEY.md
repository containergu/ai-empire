# Market Survey — Expected Revenue (AI Empire TD)

*Research date: 2026-08-30. Figures are industry benchmark ranges, not guarantees. This is a tiny, zero-marketing indie game, so set expectations accordingly.*

## TL;DR

- **Most likely: $0–$40/month** for the first few months (and that's if you get any traction).
- **If featured on a portal: $100–$600/month** for a short run.
- **Top-decile hit: $1,500+/month** — possible, but not something to plan around.
- The honest median across the whole App Store/Play ecosystem: **83% of Play apps earn under $1,000 over their entire lifetime.**

Treat this game as a portfolio/learning asset first, a revenue stream second.

---

## 1. Ad eCPM benchmarks (what one ad impression earns you)

| Ad format | eCPM (per 1,000 impressions) | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Banner | $0.50 – $2.00 | Jake Insight |
| Rewarded video (AdMob, blended) | $5 – $15 | Jake Insight |
| Rewarded video (US-heavy, web) | $15 – $28 | Cinevva |
| Rewarded video (Western iOS, 2025H1) | ~$20 | TopOn report |
| Interstitial | ~half of rewarded | Cinevva |

**Takeaway:** rewarded video is your money-maker; banner is nearly noise. US/UK/EU/JP players pay 5–10× more than Tier-3 (India/Brazil) players.

## 3. Realistic indie revenue data (Google Play, 2026)

| Monthly active users (MAU) | Typical monthly ad revenue |
|---|---|
| 5,000 MAU | $50 – $150 |
| 25,000 MAU | $300 – $800 |
| 100,000 MAU | $1,500 – $4,000 |

You need roughly **50,000+ MAU** before ad revenue feels meaningful. With no marketing budget, hitting even 5,000 MAU is the hard part — most indie games die in the "50–200 downloads in months 1–3" phase.

## 4. Revenue model for THIS game

Assumptions: rewarded video is the core (you now have 3 placements); blended net eCPM ~$10 (US-leaning); 1–2 rewarded views per active player per day.

| Scenario | Reach | Est. monthly revenue |
|---|---|---|
| 😐 Realistic (no marketing) | ~500–2,000 installs, 20–100 DAU | **$5 – $40** |
| 🙂 Featured on CrazyGames/Poki | ~100k–500k monthly plays | **$100 – $600** |
| 🤩 Hit (top decile) | 1M+ plays / 50k+ MAU | **$1,500 – $4,000** |

## 5. Bottom line & recommendation

1. **Go free + ad-supported**, with rewarded video as the engine and "Remove Ads" ($1.99) as the only IAP — this is the right model for a casual game and it's what's already built.
2. **Spend the $100 as:** $25 Google Play account, keep ~$75 as buffer (or a few paid art/icon assets). Do NOT buy paid installs at this stage — it won't be profitable on a single casual game.
3. **Highest-leverage free moves:** submit to CrazyGames + GameDistribution (their review feedback is worth more than the revenue), fix the AdMob IDs, and make the icon/screenshots look good — ASO on the listing is your only free "marketing."
4. **Don't defer learning for revenue:** a first indie game rarely pays. The realistic path to money here is *volume across many portals* and iterating based on the retention/feedback each portal gives you.
