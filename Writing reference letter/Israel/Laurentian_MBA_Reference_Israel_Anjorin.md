## Part B — Explanation of Ratings (with specific examples)

### 1. Critical Thinking & Analytical Ability

Israel demonstrated outstanding critical thinking during the CFA Research Challenge, where I served as his team's faculty advisor in Fall 2025. The team was tasked with analyzing Nike — a project requiring sophisticated financial modeling, valuation techniques, and competitive strategy assessment. Israel was instrumental in bridging academic theory with real-world financial analysis. He consistently asked probing questions about the assumptions underlying their DCF model and pushed the team to consider multiple scenarios rather than accepting a single-point estimate. His ability to synthesize complex financial data and extract actionable insights was notably mature for a graduate student.

In my GSM 5112 Economics, Finance and Markets course (Spring 2026), Israel consistently demonstrated the ability to engage with complex financial and economic concepts with clarity and confidence. His written analyses showed careful consideration of counterarguments and an ability to weigh competing explanations before arriving at well-supported conclusions.

### 2. Independence

Israel is a self-directed learner who does not require hand-holding. He came to every class well-prepared, having done the readings and formulated his own questions. In the CFA Research Challenge, while many students needed significant guidance on basic financial modeling, Israel took the initiative to learn advanced Excel modeling techniques independently and taught them to his teammates. He is capable of taking ownership of complex tasks and executing them with minimal supervision — a skill clearly demonstrated across six years of progressive accounting and financial management experience at FedEx REDSTAR EXPRESS, where he advanced from Revenue Analyst to Management Accountant without requiring constant oversight.

### 3. Perseverance

Israel's academic record speaks clearly to his perseverance. He is maintaining a 3.9+ GPA in a demanding joint MBA/MS Data Science program — one of the most rigorous combinations offered at Willamette University. He balances this with active leadership service in Rotary International (seven years, including roles as Club President and District Administrator) and recent accounting internships in the United States. Despite this demanding schedule, his work in my course was never rushed or incomplete. He approaches challenges methodically and does not give up when faced with difficulty. His career trajectory — from Revenue Analyst to Management Accountant — also reflects a steady, determined progression through increasingly responsible roles.

### 4. Originality

Israel brings thoughtful perspectives to problems rather than relying on formulaic approaches. In class discussions, he often connected course material to his professional experience in logistics and accounting at FedEx, offering real-world examples that enriched the learning environment for his peers. In the CFA Research Challenge, he suggested incorporating ESG metrics into the team's valuation framework — an approach that demonstrated awareness of evolving market standards beyond what was required. He is open-minded and intellectually curious, always willing to explore new angles.

### 5. Organizational Skills

Israel's ability to manage competing priorities is exceptional. Successfully completing a joint MBA/MS Data Science program while working internships and holding Rotary leadership positions requires precise time management and organizational discipline. His written submissions for GSM 5112 were consistently well-structured, properly formatted, and submitted on or before deadlines. In the CFA Research Challenge, he took responsibility for coordinating the team's workplan and ensuring deliverables were completed on schedule. His professional advancement at FedEx — where organizational reliability is essential — further confirms this strength.

### 6. Interest in Discovery

Israel is genuinely motivated by learning rather than merely credential-seeking. He enrolled in my GSM 5112 course because he wanted to deepen his understanding of financial markets, not because it was required. He actively participated in class discussions, stayed after class to ask thoughtful follow-up questions, and engaged with material beyond the syllabus. His pursuit of a joint MBA/MS Data Science degree itself reflects a desire to integrate business acumen with technical skills — a combination that requires intellectual curiosity and a commitment to continuous learning. His interest in pursuing graduate study in Canada demonstrates a willingness to step outside his comfort zone and engage with a new academic and cultural environment.

### 7. Communication Skills

Israel communicates with clarity and professionalism. His written work in my course was precise, logically organized, and persuasive. He articulated complex financial concepts effectively in class discussions. In the CFA Research Challenge, he was able to present technical financial analysis to both his teammates and faculty advisors in an accessible and compelling way. He is personable, humble, and engaging in interpersonal settings. He responds well to constructive feedback and incorporates it productively. If there is an area for continued growth, it would be further developing his confidence in extemporaneous public speaking — but his trajectory in this area has been consistently positive.

## Overall Assessment

Israel Anjorin is one of the most dedicated and analytically capable students I have taught. His 3.9+ GPA in a joint MBA/MS Data Science program at an AACSB-accredited school, combined with six years of progressive accounting and finance experience and seven years of Rotary leadership, makes him exceptionally well-prepared for the Laurentian MBA program. He has my highest recommendation.
