## 2. 3+2 BA/MBA Accelerated Program

**Format:** 3 years undergraduate (any major) + 2 years MBA = both degrees in 5 years
**Campus:** Salem
**Total credits:** 151–156

### Structure
- Years 1–3: Undergraduate coursework in College of Arts & Sciences
- Year 4 (senior year): Begin MBA coursework
- Year 5: Complete MBA

### Curriculum
- Follows the **ECCC (Early Career & Career Change) MBA curriculum**
- Any undergraduate major allowed (not limited to business)
- Optional MBA concentrations: finance, marketing, accounting, public & non-profit management
- Includes PACE experiential learning and internship

### Key Features
- Undergraduate financial aid applies for 8 semesters
- Charged at undergraduate tuition rate during senior year
- Alumni at Nike, Intel, Moss Adams, Boeing, Fisher Investments, Honda, Epic

**Source:** https://willamette.edu/mba/programs/eccc/formats/ba-mba/index.html

## 5. JD/MBA Dual Degree

| Detail | Info |
|--------|------|
| Duration | 4 years full-time |
| Total credits | 120 (JD + MBA) |
| MBA portion | Min 48 approved MBA credits (34 required + 14 elective, incl. 4 experiential) |
| Campus | Salem |

### Typical Sequence

**Year 1 — Law School only**
Fall: Civil Procedure, Contracts I, Lawyering, Torts
Spring: Contracts II, Lawyering, Property, Constitutional Law, Criminal Law

**Year 2 — MBA Program only**
Fall: GSM 5103 Data Analysis, GSM 5104 Managing Organizations, GSM 5105 Accounting, GSM 5108 PACE I, GSM 5111 Finance & Econ I
Spring: GSM 5107 Marketing, GSM 5109 PACE II, GSM 5112 Finance & Econ II, GSM 5114 Operations, MBA elective

**Years 3–4 — Concurrent (⅔ law + ⅓ MBA)**
- GSM 6121 Politics & Public Policy
- GSM 6123 Strategic Management
- Additional MBA electives (14+ credits total)
- Law electives (Con Law II, Evidence, Professional Responsibility, etc.)

### Business Law Certificate available.

**Sources:**
- https://willamette.edu/academics/special-programs/jd-mba/
- https://catalog.willamette.edu/programs/JD.MBA
