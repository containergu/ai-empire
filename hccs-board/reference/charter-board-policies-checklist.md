# Charter School Board Policies — Essential Checklist

> Source: [BoardSource](https://boardsource.org/resources/board-policies-checklist-charter-boards/) · 2026-05-19

---

## Eight Essential Board Policies for Charter Schools

### 1. Board Member Agreement
Defines each member's commitment — meeting/event attendance, behavioral norms, financial commitments. Reduces confusion, disengagement, or disruption.

### 2. Code of Conduct
Ethical guidance for board members, officers, employees. Framework for professional standards, reporting unethical behavior, protecting confidential information.

### 3. Conflicts of Interest
Procedures when a member's personal interests conflict with board interests. Mandates **annual disclosure** from each member. Disclosed member **must not** participate in discussion/vote on the transaction.

### 4. Document Retention & Destruction
Compliance with state/federal retention laws. Required disclosure on IRS Form 990.

### 5. FERPA Policy (Family Educational Rights & Privacy Act)
Protects student education records: grades, transcripts, attendance, discipline records.

### 6. Gift Acceptance
Which gifts the org accepts, approval levels, board review requirements. No tax advice provided.

### 7. Nondiscrimination
Commitment against discrimination: race, color, ethnicity, religion, national origin, gender, age, disability, sexual orientation.

### 8. Whistleblower
Encourages reporting illegal practices/policy violations. Protects from retaliation. Required disclosure on IRS Form 990.

---

> These supplement (not contradict) articles of incorporation and bylaws.
> Once board-approved, they carry authority similar to bylaws and contracts.
