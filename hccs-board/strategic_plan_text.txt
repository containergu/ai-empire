How should a strategic plan be used?

=== ppt/slides/slide21.xml ===
How to use the strategic planUse the strategic plan as a living document at charter school board meetings and refer to it regularly. This helps to ensure that the majority of the board’s time and energy is in alignment with the mission/vision, goals and strategies contained in the strategic plan. The strategic planning process in a charter school dovetails with "accountability planning process” or our “school improvement plan”.  Celebrate successes.Keep the strategic plan visibleDevelop a recognizable theme that signifies the plan's essential thrust. This may be graphically represented by a logo or some other visually attractive means. One charter school adopted the theme “No Excuses!” Publicize brief summaries of the plan and how your charter school will use it. Look for opportunities to refer to the strategic plan in meetings, newsletters or news releases. Publicize when important milestones in the plan are reached. A school-wide communication with graphics and charts summarizing the plan can be effective.Other ideas?

=== ppt/slides/slide4.xml ===
What is strategic planning?The process by which the guiding members of an organization envision its future and develop the necessary plans, procedures and operations to achieve it. A 3-5 year blueprint for the future.

=== ppt/slides/slide5.xml ===
Components of a strategic planThe mission, vision and goals the school will pursue  Whom you will serve  The charter school's role in the community  The kinds of programming, services or products you will offer  The resources needed to succeed  How to best combine these resources, programming and relationships for mission impact
