## Design Philosophy

The v2 designs follow the HCCS Brand Style Guide (v8/19):
- **Red + Yellow** as primary colors (the v1 designs used navy+gold which aren't official brand colors)
- Clean, straightforward aesthetic — "casual professional, crisp & accessible"
- Chinese and English treated as equal design elements, reflecting the dual-language mission
- Authentic Chinese seal/stamp motifs (望 for "Hope", 希 for "Hope/aspiration") instead of generic dragon clip art
- 3D rendering effects (gradients, specular highlights, shadows) for realistic mockups
