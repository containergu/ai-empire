# Confidentiality Agreement for Volunteers, Staff & Board Members

> Hope Community Charter School

I understand that in the course of my volunteer time with Hope Community Charter School, I may become aware of confidential information. I understand and agree that I will not disclose such confidential information except to school employees or Board members who have a need to know. I also understand that even when I am no longer a volunteer with Hope Community Charter School, any confidential information I have learned must continue to be kept confidential. I understand that any breach of these confidentiality requirements will result in my immediate termination as a volunteer and may result in legal action against me.

## Confidential Information Includes, But Is Not Limited To:

- Information about specific students, which may include such information as students’ academic performance, financial status, behavior, health, disabilities and related matters
- Donor information including names, addresses, and donation amounts, unless they have permitted disclosure
- Information shared in board or committee meetings including employment matters and ongoing negotiations (labor, purchases, or real estate)
- Employee data including staff salaries, evaluations, discipline and termination
- Pending litigations or investigations
