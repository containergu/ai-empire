Board of Directors Agreement
As a member of the Hope Chinese Charter School Board of Directors, for the term of two years from election, I agree to meet the following list of expectations: 

MISSION
Board members will be able to describe the vision, mission, and basic programs offered by Hope Chinese Charter School.

Board members will support the strategic plan of the organization and will act in accordance with that plan.

TIME
Board members will be expected to spend at least 10-20 hours per month on average providing services to Hope Chinese Charter School (HCCS) in Board meeting attendance and sub-committee participation, plus additional time in the month preceding the organization’s major fundraising event(s) and as needed.

Normal monthly time commitments include the following activities:
     -Actively participating (and being prepared for) monthly 2 hour Board meetings
     -Participating in at least one Board committee and attending related monthly meetings
     -Performing off-line action items related to these meetings

Attending and actively participating in the organization’s major annual fundraising events. 
     -For the Auction Gala, suggestions are as follows:
          -Hosting at least one table (10 Guests).
          -Helping to Secure Sponsorships.
          -Procuring auction items.
     -For the ACH Campaign, suggestions are as follows:
          -Actively Participating.

Attending other activities during the year as time allows:
     -Supporting other fundraising events 
     -Attending program events (minimum of three/year)
     -Other volunteer events

All Board Members (Directors) will evaluate the performance of the Board on an annual basis and help establish annual Board goals to which we are all held accountable.

All Directors are expected to participate in an annual all-day Board retreat.

HCCS Governing Board of Directors’ service terms are two years in length. 

ATTENDANCE/COMMITTMENT
Board members are expected to attend 75% of Board meetings either in-person or by teleconference.
Absence of any elected Board member from three consecutive meetings of the Board of Directors may be considered as cause for removal.
 
FINANCIAL
All Board members have a fiduciary responsibility to the organization:
     -Board members must understand the financial condition of HCCS, including the strength of the balance sheet and the predictability/reliability of the cash flow
     -Board members should clearly understand the budgeting process and make an informed decision when approving the budget.

Board Members are expected to contribute direct financial contributions as their financial means allow, in addition to donated time and in-kind contributions. Many granting and other community organizations evaluate the caliber of a nonprofit's Board by direct and indirect participation with financial contributions
Contribution magnitude is dependent on individual circumstances, but all members are expected to make a direct cash contribution that is of personal significance. 
Board Members are encouraged to raise at least $3,000. Contributions credited to the Board Member can include direct contributions, company matches, Gala attendees,donations by direct contacts, corporate sponsorships obtained, auction items procured, etc.

All board members will be expected to be ambassadors for the organization:
     -Developing new contacts, deepening relationships with founders and enrolling new supporters

AUTHORITY AND RESPONSIBILITY
An individual board member exercises the authority and responsibility of his/her position when the public charter school board is in legal session only.

In specific instances, a member, including the Chairperson, may have authority to act but only when the majority of the Board delegates such specific authority to him/her. In all other instances, an individual member has no power or authority.

A public charter school board member has the right to express personal opinions. When expressing such opinions in public, the board member must clearly identify the opinions as his/her own. Board members communicating outside of legal session and without authority of the Board must preface their personal opinions with: “The following is my personal opinion. I do not represent the Board”.

Members will be knowledgeable of information requested through board action, supplied by the Principal, gained through attendance at HCCS activities and through professional HCCS board activities.

TERMS
Board service is an amazing volunteer opportunity and commitment and in recognition of that and fairness to the whole organization, at the recommendation of the Board President, the Executive Committee reserves the right to terminate a member’s directorship at any time.

CONFIDENTIALITY
Board Members are expected to maintain confidentiality about HCCS matters that are of sensitive legal, financial or programmatic content.
