## Brand Voice

**"Casual professional, crisp & accessible"** — straightforward, easy for non-native speakers.

## Name Usage

| First mention | Then use | Where |
|:-------------|:---------|:------|
| Hope Chinese Charter School | **"Hope"** | Internal comms, newsletters, social media |
| Hope Chinese Charter School | **"HCCS"** | Academic papers, grants, invoices |

## Logo Rules

| Rule | Detail |
|:-----|:-------|
| **Primary** | **Horizontal** — use whenever possible |
| **Minimum width** | 1.5" for horizontal logo |
| **Color** | **Red + Yellow full color** first choice |
| **Fallback** | Black & white or greyscale only if necessary |
| **Background** | **Never** place full-color logo on red |
| **Position** | Top right or top left of page |

## Mascot — Pan Pan the Panda

- Pan Pan = "Hope" in Chinese ( near-homophone)
- Symbolizes: Panda diplomacy, peace, wisdom, creativity
- **Do:** modify pose, add props, change background
- **Don't:** put text on Pan Pan (no tattoos)
- **Don't:** use pronouns — Pan Pan has no gender
- Must remain recognizable at a glance
