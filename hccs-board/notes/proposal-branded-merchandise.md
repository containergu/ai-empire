# Proposal: HCCS Branded Merchandise Shop

**From:** George Gu, Board Member
**Date:** May 20, 2026
**Purpose:** Board discussion — new revenue stream

---

## Why This Matters

HCCS's strategic plan sets a clear goal: **"Develop new external revenue streams totaling 25% of fundraising target."** The school faces a ~$1M annual funding gap. At the same time, HCCS has a strong, distinctive brand — a dual-language Chinese immersion school — that isn't being monetized.

A merchandise program turns the school's brand into a revenue stream while building community pride and visibility.

---

## What I'm Proposing

An **online merchandise shop** — no physical store, no lease, no paid staff. Just:

1. **Print-on-demand** (zero inventory, zero risk)
2. **Batch ordering** around events (higher margins)

## Supplier Recommendations

### Print-on-Demand (zero inventory, best for starting)

| Supplier | Best For | Notes |
|:---------|:---------|:------|
| [Printful](https://www.printful.com) | Premium quality — mugs, tees, hoodies | US-based production, branded packaging, higher quality. No upfront cost. Best for building a brand. |
| [Printify](https://www.printify.com) | Best prices, wide catalog | Connects to multiple print partners so you can compare prices. Cheaper than Printful. No upfront cost. |
| [Spreadshop](https://www.spreadshop.com) | Easiest setup | All-in-one store + production. Good for non-technical users. US printing. No inventory. |
| [Amazon Merch on Demand](https://merch.amazon.com) | Marketplace reach | Amazon handles everything. Designs appear on Amazon. Best for passive sales, less brand control. |

### Batch Ordering (higher margins, event sales)

| Supplier | Best For | Notes |
|:---------|:---------|:------|
| [Custom Ink Fundraising](https://www.customink.com/fundraising) | Schools & teams | Free design help, no minimum order, bulk discounts. Dedicated school fundraising program. 7-10 day shipping. |
| [Custom Ink](https://www.customink.com) | Individual batch orders | Very easy to use. Great for one-off runs (Gala, Back to School Night, Chinese New Year). No setup fees. |

**My recommendation:** Start with **Printful** for ongoing online sales (best quality, no inventory) and **Custom Ink Fundraising** for event batch orders (higher margins, school-friendly platform).

---

## Business Model

**Phase 1 — Print-on-Demand (launch, zero risk)**
- Set up a free Printful account
- Link to a simple order page on the HCCS website or use a Google Form
- Platform prints and ships — no school staff time required
- Profit margin: 40-60%

**Phase 2 — Batch Ordering at Events (higher margins)**
- Order 50-100 pieces for Auction Gala, Chinese New Year performance, Back to School Night
- Sell at events — instant revenue
- Profit margin: 60-80%

## Sales Channels

1. **HCCS website** — add a Merch link (links to Printful store)
2. **School events** — Auction Gala, Chinese New Year, Back to School Night, graduation
3. **Tiny Dragons parents** — PreK families are highly engaged buyers
4. **WeChat parent groups** — direct link sharing

## Q&A

**Q: Does this need budget approval?**
A: No. Print-on-demand costs nothing to start. Design is the only potential cost (~$50-100 if no volunteer).

**Q: Will this burden staff?**
A: Minimal. With print-on-demand, the platform handles everything. Staff time is limited to sharing a link.

**Q: What if nothing sells?**
A: With print-on-demand, there's no inventory to write off. Zero risk.

**Q: Tax/compliance?**
A: Revenue falls under "program services revenue" similar to Tiny Dragons. Confirm with the Finance Committee.
