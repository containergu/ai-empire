# HCCS Bylaws — Board Member Summary

> Hope Chinese Charter School · Amended & Restated Bylaws (Approved 11.20.19)
> Supplemented by: Board of Directors Agreement · Ethics & Conflicts of Interest Policy (BBFA)

---

## Board Structure (Article III)

- **Size**: 5–13 directors
- **Composition requirements**:
  - At least **3** must reside in the Portland metropolitan area
  - At least **3** must be parents of a current HCCS student (excluding preschool)
  - Board collectively must have **financial and professional education expertise**
- **Term**: 2 years, unlimited successive terms
- **Staggered terms**: Directors divided into two groups at first meeting after adoption — one group serves 1-year initial term, the other 2 years, then all subsequent terms are 2 years
- **Quorum**: Majority of directors in office immediately before the meeting begins
- **Voting**: Majority of directors present in person at a quorate meeting decides the action
- **Attendance**: 3 consecutive unexcused absences = cause for removal
- **Removal**: With or without cause, by majority vote at a meeting called expressly for that purpose

## Director Qualifications & Restrictions (Article III, Sec. 3)

- All directors must be individuals
- No director may be employed by HCCS during their term of board service
- Employment of **immediate family members** of a director is **"generally disfavored"**
- Vacancies filled by majority of remaining directors (even if less than a quorum)

## Meetings (Article III, Sec. 4–9)

| Type | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Regular meetings** | Time/place set by board resolution |
| **Annual meeting** | Held in May at the principal office (unless changed by resolution) |
| **Special meetings** | Called by President or by 20% of directors then in office |
| **Notice (special)** | At least 24 hours — written, mail, or email |
| **Waiver of notice** | Attendance constitutes waiver (unless attending specifically to object to improper call) |
| **Public Meetings Law** | Must fully comply with ORS 192.610–690 |

## Officers (Article IV)

All officers **must be directors**. Appointed **annually** at the regular annual meeting.

| Office | Notes |
|--------|-------|
| **President** (also Chair) | Principal executive officer; presides at all meetings; can sign authorized instruments |
| **Vice Chair** | Succeeds President in absence/death/inability |
| **Secretary** | Minutes, notices, records custodian, director address register |
| **Treasurer** | Funds custody, deposits; may be required to post bond |

- President and Vice Chair roles: if President resigns or is removed, they are simultaneously deemed to resign/be removed as Chair, and vice versa
- Any officer can be removed with or without cause at any time
- Officer resignation requires **10 days** written notice
- Board may choose not to fill Vice Chair (or other roles except President, Secretary, Treasurer)

## Individual Board Member Authority

Per the **Board of Directors Agreement** (cross-referenced by the bylaws):

> An individual board member exercises authority **only** when the board is in legal session. No individual member (including the Chair) has power to act outside of session unless explicitly delegated by majority vote.

When expressing personal opinions publicly, board members **must** preface with:
> *"The following is my personal opinion. I do not represent the Board."*

## Conflicts of Interest (Article III, Sec. 13 + Policy BBFA)

Board members must adhere to the **Ethics and Conflicts of Interest Policy** (BBFA, adopted 7/21/21).

### Key Rules

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **No personal benefit** | May not use official position for personal financial benefit or to avoid financial detriment for self, relatives, or household members |
| **Confidential information** | May not use for personal gain |
| **Contract restriction** | If you vote to authorize a public contract, no financial interest in it for **2 years** |
| **Public officials** | Board members are bound by Oregon ethics laws for public officials (ORS 244) |

### Conflict Types

| Type | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| **Potential conflict** | Must **publicly declare**. May still vote or abstain after declaring. (Abstaining alone does **not** satisfy the declaration requirement.) |
| **Actual conflict** | Must **publicly declare**. May **not** vote (unless vote needed to reach minimum). May **not** participate in discussion or debate. |

### Gift Rules

- **$50/year max** from any single source that has a legislative or administrative interest in matters before the board
- Limit applies separately to the board member, each household member, and each relative
- HCCS-provided meals at board meetings are acceptable (expense reimbursement exception)
- **Entertainment** over $50 from a single source with legislative/administrative interest is prohibited unless incidental to another event or for ceremonial purpose
- **Honoraria** prohibited in connection with official duties

### Gifts Exceptions (not counted toward $50 limit)

1. Campaign contributions
2. Gifts from relatives and household members (unlimited)
3. Informational/program materials and subscriptions
4. Legal expense trust fund contributions
5. Reasonable travel expenses for official speaking/presentation/panel duties (from government, tribal, or nonprofit sources)
6. Food/beverage at receptions/meetings when representing HCCS
7. Food/beverage in course of financial transactions
8. Continuing education registration waivers/discounts
9. Gifts from private business practice unrelated to public office

## Committees (Article VII)

- Board may designate committees by majority resolution
- Each committee must have **one or more directors**; at least one member present at meetings must be a director
- Committees may exercise board authority **except** they cannot:
  - Authorize distributions
  - Approve dissolution, merger, or sale of substantially all assets
  - Elect, appoint, or remove directors or fill vacancies
  - Adopt, amend, or repeal articles or bylaws
- Committees must comply with **Oregon Public Meetings Law** (ORS 192.610–690)

## No Loans to Directors or Officers (Article VI)

- Loans to directors or officers are **prohibited**
- Directors who vote for or assent to such a loan are **jointly and severally liable** for the amount until repaid
- Right of contribution among directors who voted for the loan

## Compensation (Article III, Sec. 11)

- Directors receive **no salary** for board service
- **Reasonable expenses** may be reimbursed by board resolution
- Directors may serve the corporation in other (non-staff) capacities and receive compensation for those roles

## Contracts, Checks, Deposits (Article VIII)

- Board may authorize any officer or agent to enter contracts on behalf of the corporation
- Loans require board resolution
- Checks and drafts require board-determined signatures
- All funds deposited in board-selected institutions

## Books, Records & Audit (Article IX)

- Corporation must keep correct books, records of account, and minutes of board and committee proceedings
- Any director may inspect books/records at any reasonable time
- **Annual financial statement** prepared by an accountant
- **Annual audit** required per Oregon Municipal Audit Law

## Bylaw Amendments (Article XII)

Bylaws may be altered, amended, repealed, or replaced by:

> A **majority of directors present** at any regular or special meeting, **if written notice** of the intent to amend was given for that meeting.

## Dissolution (Article XV)

- No director or officer may share in distribution of assets upon dissolution
- Remaining assets distributed to similar 501(c)(3) organizations
- Assets purchased with **public funds** go to the **Oregon Board of Education**

## Indemnification (Article XIV)

- Corporation must indemnify directors, officers, employees, and agents to the **fullest extent permitted** by the Oregon Nonprofit Corporation Act
- Reasonable expenses paid in advance of final disposition
- Right to indemnification is a **contract right** between the corporation and the person
- Continues after the person ceases to serve, and inures to heirs, executors, and administrators

## Fiscal Year & Other Mechanics

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Fiscal year** | July 1 – June 30 |
| **No members** | The corporation has no members (Article II) |
| **No stock** | No shares, no dividends (Article V) |
| **Principal office** | Within Beaverton School District territory |
| **Telephone meetings** | Allowed if all participants can hear each other (Article III, Sec. 12) |
