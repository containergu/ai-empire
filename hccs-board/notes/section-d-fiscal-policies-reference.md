## Detailed Summaries

### DB — Budget
- Budget = financial plan of operation, GAAP-compliant
- Fiscal year **July 1 – June 30**
- **Administrator/principal or designee** serves as budget officer
- Legal ref: ORS 338.115(2)

### DD — Grant Funding Proposals and Applications
- HCCS may pursue federal, state, or private grants aligned with Board/school goals
- Admin reviews proposals before submission
- Board informed of submissions/awards via regular financial reporting
- Legal ref: ORS 294.305–565, ORS 338.115(2)

### DE — Revenues from Private, State and Federal Sources
- Board may authorize, accept, and use outside funds
- HCCS must comply with all regulations for receiving/using such funds
- Legal ref: ORS 294.305–565, ORS 338.115(2)

### DFA — Investment of Funds
- Board may authorize investment of funds not immediately needed
- Admin + business manager develop investment criteria
- Regular progress reports to Board
- Business manager manages donated stock acceptance
- Legal ref: ORS 338.115(2)

### DFA-AR — Acceptance of Donated Stock/Marketable Securities
- Investment account maintained for stock donations (minimum cash balance as required)
- **Stock liquidated at earliest opportunity upon receipt**
- Proceeds = unrestricted contributed income
- Report to Board: within **60 days** of donation, or **quarterly** if no donations
- Donor responsible for tax valuation (per IRS)
- Transaction cost considered small vs risk of holding

### DGA — Authorized Signatures
- At **annual organizational meeting**, Board authorizes admin/designee to sign checks
- Board may authorize facsimile signatures
- Legal ref: ORS 338.115(2)

### DIC — Financial Reports and Statements
- Board receives:
  - Expenditures vs budget appropriations
  - Actual receipts vs budget estimates
  - Overall cash condition
- Supplementary reports available upon request
- Staff available at Board meetings upon request
- Admin notifies Board of **substantial deviations** in revenues/expenditures
- Cross-ref: DIE — Audits
- Legal ref: ORS 294.155, 294.311, 297; ORS 328.465; ORS 338.095(2)

### DIE — Audits
- **Annual audit** by accountant selected by **Board** from roster of authorized municipal accountants (Oregon Board of Accountancy)
- Audit per minimum standards established by **Secretary of State**
- **Finance Committee reviews audit firm contract annually**, may solicit RFPs
- Audit report presented to Board; admin submits to **sponsor (BSD)** and **ODE**
- Cross-ref: DIC, DJB
- Legal ref: ORS 294.155, ORS 297, ORS 327.137, ORS 328.465, ORS 338.095(2)

### DJ — Purchasing
- Admin/designee = purchasing agent
- Can approve contracts up to **$5,000** within budget appropriations
- Director of ops reviews invoices; business manager directs payment
- Invoices **>$5,000 not within budget** → Board approval required
- Invoices on contracts already Board-approved and within budget → no additional Board approval
- **No obligation** may be incurred without budget authorization or Board action
- **Anti-gratuity, anti-conflict** provisions: no Board member/officer/employee may use position for financial gain; no gratuities from suppliers
- Cross-ref: BBA, BBFA, DJC

### DJ-AR — Expenditure of Funds for Meals, Refreshments and Gifts
- Meals allowed when **official school business conducted** during meal
- Refreshments ≤ **$15/participant** (gratuity ≤ 15%)
- **No alcoholic beverages** with school funds
- **No parties** with school funds
- Gifts for retirement: ≤ **$50/person**
- Social/sunshine funds (voluntary staff contributions) exempt from these rules
- Prior admin approval required

### DJB — Petty Cash Accounts
- **$100** petty cash fund
- For properly itemized bills of nominal amounts needing immediate payment
- Not to circumvent purchasing procedures
- Cross-ref: DIC, DIE

### DJC — Bidding Requirements
- **Board = Local Contract Review Board (LCRB)** for HCCS
- Adopts Oregon Attorney General's Model Public Contract Rules (OAR 137 Div 046-049)
- Procurements > **$250,000** → cost analysis and feasibility per ORS 279B
- Board must review rules each time Attorney General modifies model rules
- Cross-ref: DJ

### DJC-AR — Special Procurements and Exemptions (very detailed, 25 pages)
**Key thresholds:**
| Threshold | Requirement |
|:----------|:------------|
| ≤ $10,000 | Informal purchase |
| $10K – $150K | Informal competitive quotes (3 quotes where feasible) |
| > $150K | Formal competitive bidding or RFP |
| > $250K | Cost analysis + feasibility per ORS 279B |

**13 Special Procurement categories:**
1. Brand names / "Or equal" / Single seller / Sole source
2. Advertising contracts, purchase of
3. Advertising contracts, sale of
4. Equipment repair and overhaul
5. Copyrighted materials
6. Product prequalification
7. Requirements contracts (blanket purchase orders)
8. Used personal property/equipment
9. Information technology contracts
10. Telecommunications systems — hardware/software
11. Telecommunications services
12. Hazardous material removal / oil cleanup
13. Renegotiation of existing contracts

**6 Exemptions from competitive bidding:**
1. Brand names / Sole source
2. Product prequalification
3. Requirements contracts
4. Waiver of bid security (< $100K public improvement)
5. Waiver of performance/payment security (< $100K)
6. Projects with complex systems/components

### DLC — Expense and Tuition Reimbursements
- HCCS reimburses employees for authorized professional growth expenses
- Per IRS requirements (IRC §162)
- Admin develops AR
- Cross-ref: BHD, EEBB

### DLC-AR — Tuition Assistance and Staff Expense Reimbursement
- **$30,000 annual pool** for tuition assistance
- Max **$3,000/employee/year**
- Covers tuition only (not books, fees, etc.)
- Grade requirement: C (undergrad) / B (graduate)
- Applications reviewed 4x/year (fall/winter/spring/summer terms)
- **Per-diem meal limits:** Breakfast $14, Lunch $16, Dinner $26
- Mileage: IRS rate; lodging: reasonable commercial rates
- Out-of-state travel: admin approval; out-of-country: Board approval

### D — Disposal of Public Charter School Property (code DN)
- Board may declare property surplus and authorize disposal
- If charter terminated/dissolved: assets purchased with public funds → **State Board of Education**
- Grant-funded property: dispose per grant terms or federal/state regulations

## Key Observations for Treasurer

1. **Audit selection**: Board selects auditor (DIE); Finance Committee reviews contract annually. This is a Treasurer-led function.
2. **Budget oversight**: Admin is budget officer (DB); Board receives budget vs actual reports (DIC).
3. **Signing authority**: Board authorizes check signers annually (DGA) — verify this is on the annual meeting agenda.
4. **Purchasing limits**: Admin can approve ≤$5K within budget (DJ); anything above needs Board attention.
5. **Procurement thresholds**: $250K triggers ORS 279B cost analysis; Board as LCRB oversees bidding rules.
6. **Substantial deviations**: Admin must notify Board of material changes in revenue/expenditure (DIC).
7. **Petty cash**: Only $100 — very small, worth confirming it's sufficient.
8. **Tuition assistance**: $30K annual pool; verify it's budgeted and tracked.
9. **Investment**: Board can invest unneeded funds (DFA) — worth asking about current practice.
10. **Donated stock**: Liquidated immediately; report quarterly — verify compliance.
