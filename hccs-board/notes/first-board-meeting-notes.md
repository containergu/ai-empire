# First Board Meeting — Items to Raise

> George Gu · After July 2026 (when board term begins)

## Item 1: Annual Meeting Date Conflict

**The problem**: Two governing documents say different things.

| Document | Says | Adopted |
|----------|------|---------|
| **Bylaws** (Art. III, Sec. 4) | Annual meeting in **May** | 11/20/19 |
| **Policy BD/BDA** (Board Meetings) | Annual meeting **no later than July** | 7/21/21 |

The bylaws include *"unless changed by the board of directors"* — so the board could have changed it. But changing the annual meeting date via policy rather than bylaw amendment is questionable. Bylaws are the higher authority.

**What to ask the board**: "There's a conflict between the bylaws (May) and policy BD/BDA (July) on the annual meeting date. Which one are we following? If July, should we formally amend the bylaws to match?"

**Suggested resolution**: If the board prefers July (which makes sense — aligns with the fiscal year start), propose a bylaw amendment at a meeting with written notice per Article XII. Otherwise, revise BD/BDA back to May.

## Item 2: Minutes — Votes Must Record Each Member By Name

**The problem**: Current practice (collective "aye" on Google Meet, no individual names recorded) does not comply with policy BDDG.

**What BDDG requires** (adopted 7/21/21):

> Minutes shall include: "the results of all votes and **the vote of each member by name**"

This is also required by Oregon Public Meetings Law (ORS 192.650) — the vote of each member of a public body must be recorded by name.

**What to ask the board**: "I noticed our recent minutes record votes collectively. Policy BDDG and Oregon law require each member's vote to be recorded by name. Should we update our meeting practice so the chair calls a roll or goes around for each vote, and the secretary records individual votes?"

**Suggested fix**: Simple procedural change — for each motion, the chair asks each member individually and the secretary notes it. On Google Meet this works fine.
