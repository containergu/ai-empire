## 2. Educational Program (§5)

### Immersion Model

| Grades | Chinese | English |
|:------|:-------:|:-------:|
| K-2 | **65%** | 35% |
| 3-5 | **50%** | 50% |
| 6-8 | **35%** | 65% |

### Key Requirements
- **English Language Arts** must be taught in English by a proficient speaker (§5.1.1)
- **Curriculum** fully aligned with Oregon state standards (§5.1.7)
- **Differentiated instruction** in all classes (§5.1.5)
- Must notify District **60 days in advance** of new curriculum (§5.1.9)
- Align with **BSD Strategic Plan** (but material changes require HCCS input/approval) (§5.1.10)

---

## 3. Student Achievement (§5.7)

**The success metric:** HCCS students must meet or exceed **District dual language program** averages in ELA, math, and science.

**Safety net:** District cannot terminate for missing achievement **in a single year** if:
- (a) the *prior* year was met, AND
- (b) HCCS creates an improvement plan within 60 days

**Termination trigger:** Two **consecutive** years of failing to meet achievement standards.

## 4. Enrollment (§6)

| Rule | Detail |
|:-----|:-------|
| **Lottery** | If oversubscribed, equitable lottery. May use weighted lottery for historically underserved. |
| **Priority** | Returning students + siblings of currently enrolled |
| **Nonresident** | Can accept after all resident students placed, if space/staff available |
| **Minimum** | **25 students** active enrollment (below = termination grounds) |
| **Max** | **600 students** |

## 5. Finances (§13–14) — **Key for Treasurer Role**

### Budget
| Requirement | Deadline |
|:------------|:---------|
| Draft balanced budget submitted to District | **April 25** each year |
| Revised budget (after state funding known) | **October 15** each year |
| Report material variations (>10% or $50K on a line item) | Immediately |

### Funding from District
- **85% of General-Purpose Grant** per ADMw (§14.1)
- Same **poverty percentage** assumption as District average (§14.2)
- District pays **within 10 days** of receiving State School Fund (§14.4)
- HCCS **cannot borrow >5%** of budgeted income without Board approval (§13.5)

### Audit (§13.4)
| Item | Detail |
|:-----|:-------|
| **Required by** | ORS 338.095(2) + Oregon Municipal Audit Law (ORS 297.405–555) |
| **Auditor** | Licensed + experienced with municipal audits |
| **Final audit due** | **December 15** each year to District, ODE, and State Board of Ed |
| **Fix plan** | Within 30 days of receiving audit — address any deficiencies |
| **Consequence** | Inadequate fix plan = District may begin termination (§21.1.1) |

### Transportation (§14.12)
- HCCS pays **30%** of transportation costs
- District bills HCCS and handles ODE reimbursement

## 6. Reporting Requirements (§12)

| Report | Due | Content |
|:-------|:----|:--------|
| **Annual staff/students** | Sept 15 | Staff list, student count by grade, proof of insurance |

## 7. Board Governance (§16)

### Board Composition (§16.6)

| Requirement | Detail |
|:------------|:-------|
| **Minimum size** | **5 directors** |
| **Portland metro** | At least **3** must reside in the area |
| **Expertise** | Board must collectively have **financial + professional education expertise** |
| **Parent representation** | At least **3** must be parents of current students |
| **If below 5** | Must inform District and undertake good-faith efforts to replace |

### Board Powers (§16.1)
- Contracting, budgets, hiring/firing, facilities, student discipline
- **Final decision-making on parent complaints** (§16.1.7)
- Accepting gifts/donations/grants
- Adopting policies

### Corporate Requirements
- Must remain an **Oregon nonprofit corp** (§16.2)
- Must remain **501(c)(3) tax-exempt** (§16.5)
- Bylaws/articles cannot be amended inconsistently with this Charter (§16.2)
- Upon dissolution: assets bought with **public funds go to Oregon Board of Education** (§16.4)

## 9. Termination (§21)

### Grounds
| # | Ground |
|:-:|:-------|
| 1 | **Failure to meet any term** of this Charter or ORS 338 |
| 2 | **Failure to meet student performance** requirements |
| 3 | Failure to correct violation of federal/state law under ORS 338.115 |
| 4 | Failure to **maintain insurance** |
| 5 | **Failure to maintain financial stability** |

### Process
- **60 days** written notice with grounds stated
- HCCS may request a hearing within 30 days
- **Immediate termination** possible if health/safety endangered (§21.3)
- Appeal rights under ORS 338.105 preserved

### Dissolution (§23)
- Assets with public funds → Oregon Board of Education
- Student records → District
- HCCS responsible for winding down all debts
- District assumes **no liability** for HCCS obligations

## 10. Key Takeaways for Board Members

### What the Charter Requires of the Board
1. **Keep board ≥5 members** with financial + education expertise, ≥3 parents, ≥3 Portland-area
2. **Ensure annual audit** by Dec 15 — licensed, municipal-audit-experienced auditor
3. **Approve balanced budget** by April 25 (draft) → Oct 15 (revised)
4. **Maintain financial stability** — failure = termination grounds (§21.1.5)
5. **Cap borrowing** at 5% of budgeted income without Board approval
6. **Retain final authority on parent complaints** (§16.1.7)
7. **Respond to District requests** for board minutes, policies, member lists (§16.7)

### Critical Deadlines

| Deadline | What |
|:---------|:-----|
| **April 25** | Draft budget to District |
| **July 1** | Fiscal year starts |
| **Sept 15** | Annual staff/student/insurance report |
| **Sept 30** | Annual performance report to ODE + District |
| **October 15** | Revised budget |
| **December 15** | **Annual audit** due to District, ODE, State Board |
| **Quarterly** (Oct/Jan/Apr/Jul 25) | Financial statements + operations report |
| **Monthly** (10th) | ADMw enrollment report |

### Most Critical Risk Areas
1. **Financial stability** (§21.1.5) — explicit termination ground
2. **ESSER cliff** → budget variation must be reported (>10% or $50K)
3. **Enrollment decline** → below 25 = termination; below budget = funding gap
4. **Audit deadline** — Dec 15, cannot miss
5. **Report withholding** — up to 25% of funding withheld for late reports
