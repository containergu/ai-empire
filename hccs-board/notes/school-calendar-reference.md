## Board-Relevant Observations

### 1. Chinese New Year Break — Full Week
Feb 1-5, 2027. This is a **distinctive feature** of HCCS compared to BSD schools — the charter model allows this cultural accommodation.

### 2. Calendar Must Align with District (§10.11)
Charter requires HCCS not to hold school on any day BSD students aren't required to attend (including snow days, staff dev days, furlough days, grading days). The HCCS specific closures (CNY, etc.) are additive.

### 3. Key Budget Implications
- 173 days × enrollment determines ADMw funding
- Staff contracts are tied to this calendar
- Facility costs are calendar-independent ($585K/yr regardless)

### 4. Board Role
Board **approves** the calendar (per §16.1 — adoption of policies + oversight). The calendar is typically prepared by administration and presented to the board for adoption.
